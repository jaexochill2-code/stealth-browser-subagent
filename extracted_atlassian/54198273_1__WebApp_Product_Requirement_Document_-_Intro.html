EZBuild.AI is a web-based construction management platform designed to unify sales, operations, and finance workflows for General Contractors, Subcontractors, and Customers. It centralizes project data, budgets, invoices, documents, and communication—enhanced by an AI layer providing predictive insights, automation, and risk prevention.

High level Architecture

EZBuild.AI is organized into interconnected modules representing the full construction management workflow.
Each module shares data through a unified backend API, ensuring that updates in one area (budget or milestones) are instantly reflected in others (invoices, dashboards).

Global Navigation: Home, Dashboard, Leads, Projects, Customers, Subcontractors, Team, Settings.

Data Relationships: Leads→Projects, Projects→Budget→Invoices, Projects↔Docs/Messages/Activity,  Subcontractors↔Projects, Customers→Projects,  AI overlays all.

Navigation Hierarchy

Global Sidebar (visible to all authenticated users)

  1. Home – daily snapshot of key activities

  1. Dashboard – portfolio-level analytics and KPIs

  1. Leads – CRM pipeline with Kanban and AI follow-ups

  1. Projects – detailed project workspace with linked budgets, docs, and invoices

  1. Customers – client directory with project history

  1. Subcontractors – vendor directory and compliance tracking

  1. Team – internal users, roles, and assignments

  1. Settings – company preferences, permissions, automation rules

  1. AI Assistant (floating icon) – context-aware chat for insights and actions
