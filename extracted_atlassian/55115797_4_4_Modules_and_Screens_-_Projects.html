Project FinancialsSnapshot (Right Sidebar)

Project Financial Summary and Snapshot

The target of this section is to provide to the users’ easy insight for the project financial status and projection. This is based on 5 elements within the system:

  1. Settings – configuration of financial related parameters. Part of the setting screen.

  1. Project financial details – Project type, Sales price and number of assigned salespersons – Part of the lead details and agreement.

  1. Project milestones – expected financial projection and planning of the project’s budget. Part of the leads project definition.

  1. Budget line items – financial transactions. Actual movement of money within the project. Part of the project execution.

  1. Financial Snapshot – on the right dashboard (see Figma). Dynamic representation of the financial status on the project screen.

 

 

Settings:

The following setting is required to calculate the project financials:

  1. In the company setting we should have single field – “Lead Cost Method”, and it should have 2 options – Net or Gross. Default Gross.

  1. For each salesperson we need to have 3 parameters defined in his team management screen:

a.     Office main lead cost – percentage field. This field represents how much commission the office takes from the sale (Net or Gross according to the company definition). Range 0%-100%, default value 15%.

b.     Office referral lead cost – percentage field. This field represents how much commission the office takes from the sale (Net or Gross according to the company definition). Range 0%-100%, default value 10%.

c.     Sales commission ratio – the profit split between the sales person and the office. Always based on Net. Range 0%-100%, default value 50%.

Project financial details:

In the lead detailed and project definition we have the following relevant parameters:

  1. How many salespersons are assigned, taken from the lead or project. Can be 1 or 2.

  1. Sales price – how much is paid for the project, taken from the proposal or contract.

  1. Lead Source – referral or all the other project options.

Project milestones:

In the project milestones there’s assignment of receivables and deliverables:

·      Receivable – planned customer payments. The total of these payments should be the sales price.

·      Payables – planned expenses.

Budget line items:

Actual money transaction. What was received and expensed in this project.

Assigned to contractor, salesperson (in case of commission payment), payment for supply and others.  Payments are assigned per milestone.

Financial snapshot:

Project status is calculated according to the following formulas:

·      Sales Price – the sale value from the contract (PO)

o   Planned – the contract value.

o   Actual – sum of all the customer receivables.

Comment – some of the receivables can be reimbursement for payments done beyond the sales price.

·      Total expenses – All the expenses that the project has. Planned – according to the project milestones, actual – according to the budget line items. Excluding the lead cost or commissions.

·      Project Margin – Sales cost – Total expenses. Same here planned vs. actual.

·      Lead Cost – the commission the office takes for the lead itself. Calculated with the following formula:

If it’s Gross value:

(Office main lead cost || Office referral lead cost) * Sales price

That’s the common case.

If it’s Net value:

(Office main lead cost || Office referral lead cost) * Project Margin

Not common.

For 2 salespersons:

Office main lead cost or Office referral lead cost = average of the value for both salespersons. The formula remains the same with the average value.

·      Project Net Margin = Project Margin – Lead cost.

·      Sale commission 1 =

o   for a single sales person - Project Net Margin * Sales commission ratio of sales person 1

o   for 2 sales persons - Project Net Margin/2 * Sales commission ratio of sales person 1

·      Sale commission 2 = Project Net Margin/2 * Sales commission ratio of sales person 2

·      Office profit = Project Net Margin – Sales commission 1 – Sales commission 2

Selection Item

A Selection Item represents any material, finish, fixture, or design choice that the customer must select or approve during the project.
Examples:

·       Flooring type

·       Paint color

·       Cabinet style

·       Tile selection

·       Countertop material

·       Appliances

It is part of the project’s Selections Workflow, and is visible both internally and in the customer portal.

Purpose

Selection Items help contractors collect customer decisions in an organized way so work can progress on time and materials can be purchased.

They prevent delays caused by:

·       Missing customer decisions

·       Slow responses

·       Miscommunication

·       Wrong materials ordered
