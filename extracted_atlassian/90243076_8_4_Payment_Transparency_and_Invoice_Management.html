SCREEN 4 - PAYMENTS

Provide payment transparency.

Displayed Data:

  • Invoice number

  • Description

  • Amount

  • Due date

  • Status

Detail View:

  • Invoice breakdown

  • Attachments

  • Due date

Behavior:

  • Mark as Paid (if manual payment system used).

  • Payment progress bar updates automatically.

User Flow:
Project → View Payments → Review invoice → Pay externally → Mark Paid.
