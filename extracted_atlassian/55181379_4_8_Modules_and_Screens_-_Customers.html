AI Logic

·       Calculates Customer Health Score using metrics like payment timeliness, project delays, and dispute history.

·       Flags potential churn risk if AR overdue > 45 days or no new projects in 6 months.

·       Suggests proactive action: “Schedule review call” or “Offer renewal discount”.

UI Behavior Notes

·       Clicking a customer opens a detailed right-hand info panel.

·       AI badge color-coded:

o   🟢 Green = Healthy

o   🟠 Amber = Watchlist

o   🔴 Red = At Risk

·       List supports infinite scroll and column sorting.

User Flow

  1. User navigates to Customers → system fetches list.

  1. Selecting a row expands a side panel with summary and linked projects.

  1. AI badge updates nightly.

From within a project, clicking the customer name opens this same record for consistency.
