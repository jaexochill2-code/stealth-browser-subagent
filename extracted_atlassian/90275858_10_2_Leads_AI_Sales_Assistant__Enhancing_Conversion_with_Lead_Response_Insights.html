LEADS - AI Sales Assistant

Purpose:

Improve conversion.

Inputs:

  • Lead response time

  • Last contact

  • Budget

  • Proposal sent date

  • Historical conversion data

AI Outputs:

✔ Conversion probability %
✔ Follow-up urgency
✔ Best contact time suggestion
✔ Recommended action

Example:
“Jessica needs follow-up. Proposal sent 3 days ago.”

Actions:

  • Schedule site visit

  • Send follow-up

  • Generate estimate

  • Assign PM

Risk Layer:

If no follow-up in 48h → conversion drops 20%
→ Flag urgency
