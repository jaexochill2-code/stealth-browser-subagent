Intro

Small and medium-sized construction businesses (SMBs) form the backbone of the construction industry. These companies typically range from 1 to ~50 employees and deliver residential and light commercial projects such as home renovations, remodeling, additions, and small new builds.

Unlike large general contractors with dedicated departments, SMB construction companies operate in a highly fragmented, manual, and relationship-driven environment, where a single individual often wears multiple hats across sales, operations, finance, and compliance.

Construction is not just “building.” It is a complex coordination problem involving people, materials, regulations, schedules, payments, and trust — all managed under tight margins and constant uncertainty.

SMB project can be everything from renovating part of the house, adding ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit), replacing the kitchen, changing the plumbing and complete tear down and rebuild of a house. Different companies are handling different type of projects according to their knowledge, experience and ecosystem.

The SMB companies are usually hiring sub constructor to execute parts of their projects, and there’s high variability here between the companies, it can be one sub constructor doing all, and it can be different sub constructor for different milestones according to their expertise.

Tight tracking, timeline and inventory management are essential part execution, and this is where the general contractor experience comes into picture. More experience usually means higher tolerance for the unpredictability of this world.

Bringing projects into the company can happen in different way. potential project is referred to as a Lead. It can come from a call center, advertisement, referral of past work, and in the modern digitized work from dedicated websites based on advertisement and rating (e.g. Yelp, Google, Angy’s etc.). Lead starts from phone communication with the potential customer and the target is to get a sales man to meet the customer in the house in order to make a proposal. Once the customer approves the proposal the lead was won and it becomes a full execution project up to the project delivery.

Key Participants and Their Roles

Important - These definition are generic, and most of the time the same person is handling multiple roles and activities within SMB construction companies (and sometimes for more than one company).

General Contractor (GCC) / Contractor / Owner (Primary Decision Maker)

Often the founder and operator of the business.

Responsibilities:

  • Winning new work (sales, estimates, bids)

  • Managing crews and subcontractors

  • Communicating with clients

  • Handling permits, licenses, and compliance

  • Tracking costs, invoices, and payments

  • Resolving issues on-site and off-site

  • Highest escalation and approve path

Owner of the construction company, and usually plays also role of sales person project manager.

Office Admin / Admin / Administrator / Dispatcher

Office coordinator and backend activities for the office. Can be one person, or multiple types of admin for different part of the project life cycle (usually the breakdown is between the sales activity = lead process, and the project execution)

Responsibilities:

  • Initial customer communication and lead alignment

  • Daily tasks management and coordination

  • Calendar management for the team

  • Managing the data flow into the system repositories

  • Sometimes - financial tracking and approval

  • Tracking proposal, contracts, signature and approval

  • In most cases has the office approval authority for budget modifications

Think about her/him as the GCC right hand

Secretary / Sub admin

Depends on the office size, sometimes there will be more than one office function which will have lower authority, and will handle coordination of different task or customer communication

Responsibilities:

  • Customer callback

  • Collecting information for leads / projects

  • Coordination of tasks, calendar, meetings and approvals

Helping the Admin/GCC for administrator work

Sales / Estimator

The guy that makes the sale, meeting with the customer, reviews the needs, define the project and estimate the overall work. “Brings the job”. Each sales person has its own commission structure depends on the lead and work source.

Responsibilities:

  • Responding to leads

  • Visiting sites

  • Creating estimates and proposals

  • Following up and closing deals

Project Manager (Sometimes the Same Person)

Responsible for day-to-day project execution, sub contractor tracking, execution, delivery, quality assurance and sometimes customer communication as well.

Responsibilities:

  • Scheduling tasks and trades

  • Coordinating materials and deliveries

  • Managing timelines and changes

  • Reporting progress to clients

  • Handling delays, rework, and surprises

Subcontractors / Field Workers

Carpenters, electricians, plumbers, HVAC, painters, etc.

Responsibilities:

  • Executing the physical work

  • Coordinating schedules informally

  • Communicating changes from the field

Clients (Homeowners / Small Businesses)

Non-experts who are emotionally and financially invested.

Expectations:

  • Clear pricing

  • Timely delivery

  • Transparency and trust

  • Frequent updates

How Work Is Done Today

SMB construction runs on a patchwork of tools and habits:

  • Phone calls & text messages

  • WhatsApp group chats

  • Email threads

  • Spreadsheets

  • Paper notes

  • Personal experience and intuition

Each project becomes its own isolated system, with little structure or learning carried forward to the next job. There is no single source of truth. Everything relies on people to manage the day to day tightly and mistakes are embedded part of execution.
