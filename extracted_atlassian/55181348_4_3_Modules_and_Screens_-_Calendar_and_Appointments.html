Calendar & Appointments

Purpose

This module allows users to schedule, view, and manage all lead-related appointments, including site visits, consultations, and follow-ups.
It integrates directly with team calendars, supports availability overlays, and leverages AI to suggest optimal time slots and reduce scheduling conflicts.

Key Capabilities

·       Weekly, monthly, and list calendar views.

·       AI-driven scheduling recommendations.

·       Real-time team availability visualization.

·       One-click creation of new appointments.

·       Conversion from appointment → project.

·       Calendar integration (Google / Outlook).

Appointment Cards

Each appointment displays:

·       Lead name

·       Appointment type (Site Visit / Estimate / Follow-up)

·       Assigned team member avatar

·       Time range

·       AI color code:

o   Teal – Site Visit

o   Amber – Estimate

o   Gray – Follow-up

o   Red – Canceled

Hover → Preview popup with details (location, participants, notes).
Click → Opens Appointment Detail Modal.

AI & Automation Panel (Right Sidebar)

Section

Function

Displays total appointments, conversion rate, and breakdown by type.

 

Team Availability Overlay

·       Toggle “Show Availability” to view colored bars representing team workload.

·       Each team member is assigned a unique color.

·       Hover tooltip shows: “Name, Current Load %, Next Available Slot.”

·       AI can highlight earliest overlapping free slot between multiple members.

·       Integration with Team Management and external calendars (Google/Outlook).

Example Workflow

  1. Sales rep creates a new appointment from a lead.

  1. AI suggests best time slot (e.g., “Tomorrow 2:00 PM, Michael & Laura available”).

  1. Appointment confirmed and synced to calendars.

  1. 24h reminder sent automatically.

After visit, rep adds notes → AI prompts conversion to project.
