TEAM MEMBERS (GC / PM / Sales)

Definition

Internal operational users with permission-based access.

Permissions vary per user.

Examples:

  • GC Admin (full access)

  • PM (project-level)

  • Sales (lead-level

Example

Category

Capabilities

Leads

View, create, update, schedule, convert

Projects

View status, create, update timeline, assign tasks

Tasks (Checklist)

Create, modify, reassign

Calendar

Schedule, reschedule

Invoices

View, approve (GC only)

Risk

View risk alerts

Communication

Message any internal role

Reports

Request summaries

Sales:

  • “Show high probability leads.”

  • “Schedule site visit tomorrow 3pm.”

  • “Generate proposal for Michael Brown.”

PM:

  • “Project risk Oakridge.”

  • “Assign plumbing task to Mike.”

GC:

  • “Cash flow next 60 days.”
