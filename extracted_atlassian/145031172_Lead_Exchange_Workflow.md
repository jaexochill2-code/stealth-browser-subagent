$$\text{Publish} \longrightarrow \text{Match} \longrightarrow \text{Claim} \longrightarrow \text{Earn}$$

---

## 2. Publish Reasons

Contractors publish leads to the EZBuild Lead Exchange under four primary trigger conditions:

1. **Outside Service Area**: The prospective lead's geographic location is beyond the contractor's operational range.
2. **Wrong Project Type**: The scope of work does not match the contractor's specific specialization or license tier.
3. **Capacity Overload**: The contractor's current crew schedules or project queues are at maximum capacity.
4. **Budget Mismatch**: The project scope or customer budget falls below the contractor's minimum engagement threshold.

---

## 3. Core Benefits Breakdown

### For Contractors:
* **Earn Referral Revenue**: Monetize unserviceable or rejected leads that would otherwise be discarded, generating passive referral income.

### For EZBuild Platform:
* **Platform Commission**: Capture a transactional percentage/fee upon successful matching, claiming, and conversion of shared leads.

### For the Network:
* **Better Lead Utilization**: Ensures high conversion efficiency across the entire contractor marketplace, matching homeowners and project owners with available, qualified contractors.
